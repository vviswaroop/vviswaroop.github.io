# Portrait treatment

Created using the built-in image generation tool from the photo supplied by the site owner. The original photo is not included in the site. The source artwork is in `src/assets/viswaroop-print-portrait.png`; Astro generates optimized WebP versions for the homepage and About page.

## Generation prompt

Use case: style-transfer, identity-preserving portrait edit. Input image 1 is the edit target: the supplied photograph of Viswaroop. Make one finished standalone square portrait asset for his personal engineering atlas website. Crop to head and upper chest, preserve the exact person's recognizable facial proportions, dark wavy hairstyle, beard, warm smile, and white open-collar shirt. Transform the photograph into a sophisticated digital fine-art print: restrained oil-painted tonal shapes combined with fine laser-etched crosshatching and subtle ink grain. Clearly illustrated rather than a photo with a filter; realistic faithful face, not caricature. Limited palette of deep forest green #183e32, warm paper #eeeee7, muted terracotta/rust accents, retain natural warm brown skin tones using the same palette harmoniously. Remove the yard, buildings, fence and other subjects from the background; use a quiet flat warm-paper ground with subtle print texture. Face should occupy about half the image height, hair comfortably uncropped, balanced shoulders near lower edge. Art directed, human, tactile, crisp detail readable at 200px. No text, frame, badges, symbols, collage, neon lasers, futuristic effects, or watermark. One image only, not a contact sheet.
